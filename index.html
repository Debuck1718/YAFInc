Who we are
01 / Our story

Education opens doors.
Leadership changes
what’s on the other side.

Youth and Future Incorporated (YAFInc) is a Ghanaian non-profit building a generation of transformational leaders. For nearly fifteen years, we’ve created spaces where young people find their voice, discover their potential and turn ideas into action. We believe education and good health are the foundations every thriving future is built on.
